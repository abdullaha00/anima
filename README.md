Cairn

A proactive palliative care system that flags deterioating patients and arranges conversations with the relevant team in advance, so that patients can die with a dignified and respectful care plan.

Cairn does three things: it finds the patients who are dying but have no plan (proactive case-finding across a simulated NHS dataset using recognised tools like SPICT/GSF and frailty and admission signals), it prepares the clinician for the conversation with a structured, patient-specific guide, and it turns the resulting conversation into a coded ReSPECT/ACP record that travels to everyone who needs it — GP, out-of-hours, ambulance, hospice, family. We're not building the scribe; Cairn is the clinical intelligence wrapped around it.
